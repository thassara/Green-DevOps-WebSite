Green DevOps - Energy efficient CI/CD Pipieline 
R26-SE-027
‹2›
The Real-World Problem 
Rapid growth of cloud-native systems and CI/CD pipelines creates green debt.
DevOps pipelines prioritize speed over energy efficiency.
Small code changes trigger full builds and tests.
This causes resource waste, high energy usage, and increased carbon emissions. [1][2]
Credible Evidence
Carbon Footprint: ICT sector produces 2–4% of global CO₂ emissions.[1]
Resource Waste: About 90% CPU time wasted during full rebuilds.[2]
Economic Impact: 35% annual increase in cloud costs.[1]
